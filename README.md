# AI-Based Interview Training and Proctoring System

## 📌 Project Overview

The AI-Based Interview Training and Proctoring System is an AI-powered platform designed to help students and job seekers prepare for real-world interviews through intelligent simulations, automated evaluation, and secure online proctoring.

The system creates personalized interview experiences based on a candidate's resume and provides detailed feedback on technical knowledge, communication skills, confidence levels, and overall interview performance.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many students struggle during placements and recruitment processes due to:

- Lack of realistic interview practice
- Limited access to personalized feedback
- Low confidence during actual interviews
- Difficulty identifying weak areas
- Unfair practices during online assessments

These challenges often prevent capable candidates from performing at their full potential.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

This project provides a complete AI-powered interview preparation ecosystem that simulates real recruitment processes while maintaining assessment integrity through intelligent proctoring.

The platform offers:

- Resume-based interview generation
- AI-powered performance evaluation
- Real-time feedback and analysis
- Multi-round interview simulations
- Emotion and confidence assessment
- Secure online proctoring

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📄 Resume Analyzer

- Analyzes candidate resumes
- Extracts skills, projects, and experience
- Generates personalized interview questions
- Creates role-specific interview scenarios

### 🎓 Daily Training Module

Candidates can practice daily through:

- Aptitude Assessments
- Technical Question Practice
- HR Interview Preparation
- Communication Skill Development

### 🎤 Multi-Round Interview System

The interview process mimics real hiring workflows:

#### Round 1 – Screening Round
- Aptitude Assessment
- Technical MCQs
- Initial Candidate Filtering

#### Round 2 – Technical Interview
- Resume-Based Questions
- AI-Generated Follow-Up Questions
- Technical Knowledge Evaluation

#### Round 3 – HR Interview
- Behavioral Questions
- Communication Assessment
- Personality Evaluation

### 🤖 Real-Time AI Feedback

The system evaluates:

- Answer Quality
- Technical Accuracy
- Communication Skills
- Confidence Levels
- Response Effectiveness

### 👀 AI Proctoring System

Advanced monitoring features include:

- Face Detection
- Eye Tracking
- Multi-Face Detection
- Tab Switching Detection
- Continuous Session Monitoring
- Automated Warning System

### 😊 Emotion Detection

The platform analyzes:

- Confidence Levels
- Stress Indicators
- Candidate Engagement
- Behavioral Patterns

### 📊 Performance Dashboard

After every interview session, the system generates a detailed report containing:

- Technical Score
- Confidence Score
- Integrity Score
- Selection Probability
- Strength Analysis
- Weakness Identification
- Personalized Improvement Suggestions

---

## 🏗️ System Workflow

1. Candidate uploads resume
2. AI analyzes resume content
3. Personalized interview questions are generated
4. Candidate attends simulated interview rounds
5. AI evaluates responses in real time
6. Proctoring module monitors candidate activity
7. Emotion detection tracks confidence and engagement
8. Detailed performance report is generated
9. Improvement recommendations are provided
